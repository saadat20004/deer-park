  1. Drive along Elk Park Road and look for two big willow trees. They mark the entrance.
  2. From Highway 206, take the second entrance. From Middle Road, take the first. Both lead to the same gravel driveway.
  3. Turn right straight away onto the site beside the hookup point. Big rigs: come in from Middle Road so the site is on your left, drive past the exit, and back in close to the hookups.
The turn can be tight for larger rigs. Watch for dips on both sides of the driveway and low branches.
